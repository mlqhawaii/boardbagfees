What makes an airline surf-friendly?

Is the cheapest airline always the best for surfboards?

No. A low fee can be offset by short length limits, per-board charges, route exceptions or inconsistent handling. Compare the whole policy.

Are these ratings the same as traveler ratings?

No. BoardBagFees surf ratings summarize the published policy. Traveler ratings are separate and come from approved firsthand reports.
