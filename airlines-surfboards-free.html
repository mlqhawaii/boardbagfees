When “free” really means included

Some airlines may accept a surfboard as part of the normal checked-baggage allowance instead of charging a separate sports-equipment fee. That can still depend on fare, weight, dimensions, route and operating carrier.

Always verify the details.
“Included” does not mean unlimited. Oversize or overweight charges can still apply.
