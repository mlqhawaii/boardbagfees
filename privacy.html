Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Privacy summary: BoardBagFees uses analytics to understand site usage and stores information you voluntarily submit in traveler reports. Do not include sensitive personal information in a report.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 and Vercel Web Analytics to understand traffic, page views, device/browser information, referral sources, and interactions such as policy-link clicks. These services may process technical identifiers and usage information according to their own privacy practices and your browser or consent settings.

Traveler reports

If you submit a traveler report, we may store the airline, rating, route, trip date, fee, display name, comment, submission time, and moderation status. Reports are reviewed before publication. Do not submit passport numbers, booking codes, payment card information, home addresses, or other sensitive information.

Security and retention

Administrative credentials and database secret keys are kept server-side. We retain information as reasonably needed to operate, moderate, secure, and improve the service, subject to applicable law.

Your choices

You can use browser privacy controls, content blockers, or other available settings to limit certain analytics technologies. Blocking analytics does not prevent ordinary access to BoardBagFees.

Updates

We may update this notice as our tools and practices change.
